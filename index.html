Lisan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Lisan is a Classical Arabic learning app. Here's what actually happens to your data.

No account, no server

You don't sign up or log in, and there's no backend server storing anything about you. The app just runs on your phone. There's no account to collect anything into.

Your progress stays on your device

Words you know, your review history, streaks, settings, the languages you told the app you speak — all of it lives in the app's local storage (IndexedDB) on your phone and nowhere else. It's never sent anywhere. Uninstall the app or clear its data and it's gone for good, including for us.

Optional AI translation, using your own API key

In Settings you can paste your own Anthropic API key to get AI-simplified translations of dictionary entries. If you turn this on:

  • The key stays on your device.
  • The dictionary text you're looking up gets sent straight from your phone to Anthropic's API using that key. It doesn't pass through us.
  • Skip this entirely and the app still works — you just won't get the AI-simplified translations.

What Anthropic does with that request is covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

Dictionary lookups

Looking up a word's classical dictionary entry pulls from arabiclexicon.hawramani.com, a public lexicon site, through the app so results can be cached on your phone for offline use later. The only thing sent is the Arabic root you're looking up — no personal info attached.

What Lisan doesn't do

  • No ads or ad tracking.
  • No analytics, no usage tracking.
  • Nothing to sell or share, because nothing leaves your device in the first place.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Permissions

Just INTERNET, used only for the two optional things above: your own Anthropic key and dictionary lookups.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, this page gets updated and the date at the top moves.
