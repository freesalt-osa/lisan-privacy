Lisan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Lisan ("the app") is a Classical Arabic learning app. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

No account, no server of ours

Lisan does not require you to sign up or log in, and it does not have a backend server that stores your data. There is nothing for us to collect, because there's no "us" in the loop during normal use — the app runs entirely on your device.

Where your progress lives

Everything about your learning progress — words you know, review history, streaks, settings, your declared spoken languages/dialects — is stored locally on your device only, in the app's local browser storage (IndexedDB). It is never transmitted anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this progress is permanently deleted and we have no copy of it.

Optional: your own Anthropic API key

Settings lets you optionally paste your own Anthropic API key to unlock AI-assisted translation of dictionary entries. If you choose to do this:

  • The key is stored locally on your device only.
  • When you use this feature, the relevant dictionary text is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) using your key — not through any server we operate.
  • This is entirely optional. The app works fully without it.

Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy, not this one.

Dictionary lookups

Looking up a word's classical dictionary entries may fetch data from arabiclexicon.hawramani.com, a public lexicon source, proxied through the app so results can be cached on your device for offline reuse. No personal information is sent with these requests beyond the Arabic root being looked up.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No sharing or selling of data — there's no data of yours on our side to share.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Permissions the app requests

INTERNET (for the optional features above) and, on older Android versions only, scoped storage access to let you export progress to your Downloads folder. Nothing else.
