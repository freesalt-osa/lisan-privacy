Qamous — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Qamous is an Arabic learning app. It was previously called Lisan. Here is what actually happens to your data.

No account, no server

You do not sign up or log in, and there is no backend server storing anything about you. The app runs on your phone. There is no account to collect anything into.

Your progress stays on your device

The words you know, your review history, streaks, settings, your search history, your notebook, and the languages you told the app you speak all live in the app's local storage on your phone and nowhere else. None of it is sent anywhere. Uninstall the app or clear its data and it is gone for good, including for us.

The optional parts that use an API key of your own

Most of Qamous runs entirely on your device. Three things cannot, because they need a language model, and you turn them on by adding an API key of your own in Settings. You choose the provider: Google Gemini, Anthropic, or OpenRouter.

  • Conversation. The text of the conversation you are having is sent from your phone to the provider whose key you added, so that it can answer what you actually said.
  • Writing down what you said. If you use the Speak button, the audio recording is sent to that same provider to be transcribed, and the text comes back to your phone. The recording is not kept after that, and it is not sent anywhere else.
  • AI help on a word or a passage. The text you asked about is sent, and the answer comes back.

In every case the key and the request go straight from your phone to that provider. They do not pass through us, because there is no server here for them to pass through. What the provider then does with the request is covered by their own privacy policy, not this one: Google, Anthropic, OpenRouter.

Add no key and none of this happens. Everything else in the app still works.

The microphone

The app can record you, in the pronunciation practice and in the conversation, and only when you press a record button. What happens to the recording depends on where you are:

  • In pronunciation practice it is compared with the model pronunciation on your phone and played back to you. It does not leave the device.
  • In the conversation, and anywhere the app writes down what you said, it is sent to the provider whose key you added, as described above. Without a key, that button is not shown.

Nothing is recorded in the background, and no recording is stored after you leave the screen.

Listening recordings

The spoken recordings in the Listening section are downloaded from the app's own static hosting when you open one, and cached on your phone so they work offline afterwards. Nothing about you is attached to that request.

What Qamous does not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics and no usage tracking.
  • Nothing to sell or share, because nothing about you is collected in the first place.
  • No access to your contacts, your location, your camera, or your photos.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: downloading the listening recordings, and the optional requests to the provider whose key you added.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: the record buttons described above, and only while you are pressing one.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: so a recitation or a recording keeps playing when the screen goes off.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: the playback notification, with its play and skip controls.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: only to show the system dialog asking for it, because some manufacturers stop playback even with a foreground service running. It grants nothing by itself, and declining changes nothing except that playback may be cut short.

Children

Qamous is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
